# ESL Salaries in Korea 1995 - 2035

These charts visualise the salaries of ESL teachers in Korea from 1995 until today and beyond. The goal is to make a comparison over time and with the median Korean income, the Korean minimum wage and the US median salary.

The data was compiled using Korean and US government statistics, news articles, job adverts, Reddit discussions, and AI estimates. More recent data is more reliable.

Future estimates are based on growth rates over the past 10 years.
